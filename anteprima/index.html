FAQ

Good to know

What if I get injured or can't race?

The deposit is non-refundable, but you can move it once to another race within 12 months, up to 3 days before your race. If I have to cancel, you get a full refund.

When do I pay the rest?

Right after the race, by payment link or QR code. Or pay in full when you book and forget about it.

What if you're filming someone else that day?

I take at most one athlete per division start block and 5 a day, so I can be there for your whole race.

How do I get my clips?

Through a private link, sent by email as soon as they're ready.

Can you film doubles or relay teams?

Yes. Prices on request: tell me about your team when you book.

Do you travel for races?

Yes: I've filmed at HYROX Stockholm, Istanbul and Rimini. Check the upcoming races or tell me yours.
